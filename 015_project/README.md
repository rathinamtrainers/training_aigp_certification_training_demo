# The project — the client, and the whole arc of the course

These two documents are the course as a whole. Everything in a `use_case_NN/` folder is
one hour out of this.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`project.md`](project.md) | **The client.** Kabini Workforce Services — who they are, the four AI systems, the people the room deals with, their existing systems and data, the architecture of the pack the course builds, the law and standards it rests on, what a participant must have installed, and what is deliberately out of scope |
| [`use_case_plan.md`](use_case_plan.md) | **The arc.** All thirty use cases in order, each with what it delivers and what it teaches, and then each one in detail |

## Read `project.md` first

Every document in this repository quotes Kabini facts and expects you to know them. It is
also where the honest disclaimer lives: **Kabini is a teaching client.** Kabini Workforce
Services B.V., MatchScore, Nora, Cadence, Attestra Inc., Halcyon AI and the named people
were authored for this course and exist nowhere else. Facts about the AIGP certification
and its Body of Knowledge are real, quoted from the extracted IAPP text and located by
line.

## The plan is a plan

`use_case_plan.md` says what each of the thirty hours is meant to deliver. It is the
teaching order and the reason for it, not a promise about the pace: how far a room gets in
a sitting is not knowable in advance, and a use case is carried across as many sittings as
it takes. The `Status` column is the trainer's own bookkeeping about what has been built,
and it says nothing about what your class has covered.

## Where the paths in these documents point

Both quote paths like `005_research/2026-09-07/report.md` — the dated research behind the
course. That material stays in the training's research repository and is not here. The
paths are kept as written so a citation can be checked against its source rather than
quietly rewritten into something that resolves nowhere.

This folder is a copy, refreshed on each delivery. Edits here are removed; the originals
live in research.
