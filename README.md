# AIGP Certification Training — student repository

*Rathinam Trainers & Consultants · The AI Engineering Series*

This is the repository the class clones. It holds what a participant is shown in the
room and can work through afterwards at their own desk: the deck as a PDF, the concept
pages taught before each section, and the section itself as it was delivered.

**It is not the working repository.** The topic research, the brochure, the curriculum,
the project plan and the use-case backlog live in the training's *research* repository,
and the trainer's own copies live in the *rehearsal* one. Material arrives here by a
delivery step, one use case at a time, and each `use_case_NN/` folder is **regenerated
from scratch** on every delivery — so a file edited here by hand is a file the next
delivery removes. Corrections belong upstream in research.

## What this course builds

Kabini Workforce Services is a teaching client. Across the course the room builds one
thing for it — the **Kabini AI Governance Pack** — a section at a time, and each use case
delivers a section that is written out, filled in, dated, owned and sourced rather than
sketched. Kabini, MatchScore, Nora, Cadence, Attestra Inc., Halcyon AI and the named
people are authored material and exist nowhere else; facts about the certification and
its blueprint are quoted from the IAPP Body of Knowledge and located by line.

**Nothing in this course installs, runs or needs a key.** Every deliverable is Markdown,
read in any editor or put on a projector. That is a decision about the audience, not a
shortcut: the artefacts are the kind of document a governance professional signs.

## What a use-case folder holds

`slides/slides.pdf` is the deck as it was projected. `concepts/` is the small teaching
pages that come *before* the section — one idea each, taught so the room can follow what
follows. The section's own folder holds the deliverable and its annexes. Where a use case
has a hand-written walkthrough it sits at the top of the folder as `PRACTICE.md`; where
there is none, none has been written yet, and nothing here generates one.
